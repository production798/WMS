Rkive WMS

Put the WMS on your phone's home screen. It opens with one tap, straight to the login page.

Your phone asks you to confirm. The Rkive WMS icon then appears on your home screen.

Add to your iPhone

  1. Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari (top right on iPad).
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen .
  3. Tap Add. The Rkive WMS icon appears on your home screen.

Open this page in Safari to do this. In other browsers, open it in Safari first.

Add to your phone

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu at the top right.
  3. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen, then Install.
